Numbering

:numbered: numbers everything it reaches, across document boundaries, and the numbers appear both here and as prefixes on the target pages' headings. The third chapter also writes its own .. sectnum::, which this toctree's numbering overrides entirely — see that page for why.

Globs and ordering

:glob: expands a pattern against the project's documents, sorted; a * does not cross a /. :reversed: flips the order after expansion.

  • Globbed Beta
  • Globbed Alpha

Hidden

A :hidden: toctree renders nothing here but still contributes to the sidebar, the reading order and the section numbering. Its :name: makes it a reference target: hidden-toc.

orphan.rst is deliberately listed by no toctree, and says so with an :orphan: field of its own — removing that field is what makes the build warn about it.
