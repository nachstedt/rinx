Dropdown Markers and Animation

:chevron: chooses which arrow marks the open/closed state. The value names the pair of directions the marker moves between, and only the closed half is drawn — the stylesheet rotates it on open.

right-down (the default)

The marker points right when closed and down when open. Writing no :chevron: at all gives the same result.

down-up

The marker points down when closed and up when open.

:animate: reveals the body with a transition instead of an instant jump.

fade-in

The body fades in.

fade-in-slide-down

The body fades in while sliding down.
